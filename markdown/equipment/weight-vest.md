A fitted vest loaded with plates or weighted inserts that adds load while keeping the hands free. Standard weights range from a 14/20 lb pair up to 45 lb plate carriers. Worn for running, pull-ups, push-ups, step-ups, and hero-style workouts to raise intensity without changing the movement.
