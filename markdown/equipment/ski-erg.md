A standing ergometer (Concept2 SkiErg) that resists a double-poling pull of two handles down and past the hips, mimicking Nordic ski technique. It is a low-impact, trunk-dominant cardio piece measured in meters or calories and pairs well with running and rowing in mixed workouts.
