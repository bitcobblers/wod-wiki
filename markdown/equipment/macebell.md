A long steel mace — a slender handle with a small, heavy ball at one end — descended from the ancient Persian and Indian war mace. The extreme offset load makes 360s, 10-to-2 swings, and gravediggers a masterclass in core anti-rotation, grip, and shoulder stability; start light and switch sides evenly.
