An indoor rowing ergometer (the Concept2 is the gym standard) driving a flywheel through a handle and chain. Rowing is a full-body stroke — legs, then back, then arms out; arms, then back, then legs back — measured in meters or calories on the monitor, with damper settings adjusting the feel of the pull.
