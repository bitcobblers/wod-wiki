A long steel bar loaded with weight plates, secured by collars. The standard men's Olympic barbell is 7.2 ft (2.2 m) and 20 kg (45 lb); women's bars are shorter and 15 kg. Used for squatting, pressing, deadlifting, and the Olympic lifts, typically set up on a squat rack or lifting platform.
