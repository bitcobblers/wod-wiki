A soft, roughly basketball-sized weighted ball used for explosive throwing, catching, and squat-to-press patterns. The classic medicine-ball clean and the wall-ball shot — throwing at a 9 or 10 ft target — are gym staples; choose a load you can keep moving with clean mechanics.
