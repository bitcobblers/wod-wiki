A dense, non-bouncing ball with a tough shell, designed to be slammed into the floor at full force. Unlike a rebounding medicine ball it dead-bounces, so every rep requires lifting the ball again from the ground; heavier d-balls (30–150 lb) also serve as odd-object carries and loading.
