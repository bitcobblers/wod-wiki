A bench-style apparatus with a foot plate, hip pad, and adjustable hooks for the ankles, built around hip-extension and back-extension arcs. GHD sit-ups and glute-ham raises train the trunk through a long range of motion — build volume gradually, as the long-lever sit-up is notorious for leaving newcomers sore.
