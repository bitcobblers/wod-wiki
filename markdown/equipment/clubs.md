Weighted club-shaped implements swung in circular and pendular patterns. Light Indian clubs (1–2 lb) drive shoulder mobility and rhythm, while heavy clubs (5–45 lb) load casts, mills, and shields for rotational strength; the offset leverage makes modest weights feel far heavier than dumbbells of the same mass.
