A stationary cycle ergometer (the Concept2 BikeErg is the gym standard) with a weighted flywheel, damper, and a road-bike-style crank and saddle position. Unlike a fan bike it is legs-only, with cadence and power shown on the monitor, making it the choice for controlled cycling intervals in meters or calories.
