A thick hemp or synthetic rope suspended from the ceiling, commonly 15 ft tall in CrossFit gyms. Climbed hand-over-hand — with or without leg assistance — or descended for eccentric work; legless climbs shift the demand fully to the upper body. Grip and technique matter more than pure pulling strength.
