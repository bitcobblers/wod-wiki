# Scene 1 — The Problem
*Slot: 1 of 7 · Timeline 0:00–0:13 · Runtime 13s*

**Job of the scene:** pure pain, no product. Fragmented tools, their structure, their
servers. The three-panel split at the end is the image Scene 2 answers.

## Narration script (32 words ≈ 13s)
*Delivery: matter-of-fact, slightly weary. Pause a beat before "So you plan…"*
> Most workout apps squeeze your training into their structure — and keep your data on
> their servers. So you plan in one app, run it in another, and decode it in a
> spreadsheet.

## Animation spec (second by second)
- **0:00–0:02** — Board is empty (app background color). A generic gray app window
  draws itself center-frame (stroke-draw, 1s), then fills flat. No wod.wiki styling —
  this is deliberately *not* the product.
- **0:02–0:05** — Athlete (CHAR-1, pose P1) fades in at the window's left, seated. He
  clicks the `Select movement…` dropdown; a list opens; he scrolls it twice; the list
  snaps shut. Shoulders drop 4° (the slump — posture only, face unchanged).
  *VO sync: "squeeze your training into their structure."*
- **0:05–0:07** — A wizard dialog scales in over the window: `Step 4 of 9`, a progress
  bar at 40%, grey `Next` button. Marker hand (CHAR-2) enters from bottom-right, one
  diagonal cross-out stroke over the wizard, exits. Dialog dims to 40%.
- **0:07–0:09** — The window's workout card slides up-right into a generic cloud icon
  with a padlock. Card disappears inside; padlock clicks shut (small scale pop).
  *VO sync: "keep your data on their servers."*
- **0:09–0:12** — Hard rearrange: window, cloud, and dialog slide apart and re-form as
  three separate panels across the board — `PLAN` (mini calendar), `TIMER` (standalone
  clock face), `SHEET` (grid with hand-typed numbers). Between panels 2 and 3, a
  circular sync arrow spins once and stops with a small ✗.
  *VO sync: "plan in one app… run it in another… decode it in a spreadsheet"* — each
  panel pulses once as its word lands.
- **0:12–0:13** — All three panels shrink to 60% and dim to 50%. Marker annotation
  writes under them: "three apps, three formats." Hold. Cut on settled frame.
