2. Polarity states, Final 5s color shift, and clock layout jitter

Captured live on Minimal at 390x760 running 0:09 Bike Sprint followed by *:30 Rest. Follow the red dashed reference line across the first three screens at y = 366px: because TimerPolarityBadges returns null during normal work and mounts a pill row plus a 1px card border at 0:05, the clock jumps down by 13.5px right as the sprint finishes.

1Digit color polarity in timerPolarityVisuals.tsx

Complexity: Low · Value: High · Files: SessionClockProjection.ts:105, timerPolarityVisuals.tsx:17

Switching digit tokens between text-foreground, text-destructive in the final 5 seconds, text-metric-rest during rest, and dimmed muted text when paused gives instant room-scale feedback. Because interpolateSessionClockFrame recomputes polarity from spans, Chromecast gets the exact 5-second color transition at 60fps without network chatter.

2Polarity pills and tinted card borders

Complexity: Low to Medium · Value: Low · Files: TimerCore.tsx:128, TimerStackView.tsx:324

Both hero components carry explicit layout-stability comments promising that state changes never shift the clock. Conditionally mounting TimerPolarityBadges and adding a 1px border only on non-work states breaks that rule right at 0:05. In addition, timerPolarityVisuals.tsx:44 maps 'overrun' to the label "Complete". Dropping the pill strip and border tint, or reserving fixed height for the strip in every state, eliminates the jump.

3Proactive Locked badge and mandatory RestBlock lock

Complexity: Low · Value: Medium · Files: RestBlock.ts:95, GenericTimerStrategy.ts:56

Showing Locked up front on required countdowns is clearer than letting athletes tap Next and wonder why nothing happened. However, RestBlock.ts:95 hardcoded required: true on every injected rest block rather than reserving skip-locks for explicitly required timers such as *:30 Rest, and stacks two pills side by side during every rest interval.

4Clipped skip-attempt flash on Minimal and General

Complexity: Bug fix, 2 lines · Files: TimerCore.tsx:64, TimerCore.tsx:107

In TimerCore.tsx, SkipFlash is rendered as a direct child of the outer overflow-hidden hero container with absolute -top-2 -translate-y-full. That places the warning toast at negative Y coordinates above the clipped box, making "Timer can't be skipped!" completely invisible on phones, tablets, and TVs when tapping Next on a locked countdown.

3. Detail tier unification, live telemetry, split deltas, and Script Outline

Captured live at 1280x820 during Round 2 of Helen Benchmark on 21 Kettlebell Swings 24 kg. Inspect the zoomed left rail on the right to see both the new round split deltas and two concrete bugs side by side.

5Typed summary metric bubbles and 0px collapse

Complexity: Medium, net -56 lines in MetricTrackerCard.tsx · Value: High

Replacing string-regex metric guessing with typed ProjectionResult objects from AnalyticsEngine cleaned up MetricTrackerCard.tsx and brought live session aggregates onto Chromecast. Adding empty:hidden reclaims the dead border strip when no summaries exist. On narrow 390px phones, however, 6 bubbles wrap onto 3 rows and consume roughly 160px of height.

6Round-over-round split deltas in RuntimeStackView

Complexity: High, ~180 lines · Value: Medium to High · Files: OutputEmitter.ts:409, LiveSessionTelemetry.ts:112

Athletes care whether Round 2 was slower than Round 1, so the +1s delta badge is genuinely useful. Two defects need cleanup: LiveSessionTelemetry.ts:130 formats labels from all non-time metrics, dumping enriched analytics like 243.01 m/s 0.07 min/km 0.27 min into the row text, and VisualStateComponents.tsx:351 slices the global telemetry.splits.slice(-4) on every nested block past index 0.

7Static Script Outline checklist in VisualStatePanel

Complexity: Medium, +180 lines · Value: Low · Files: ScriptOutlineProjection.ts, visual-state-panel.tsx:32

VisualStatePanel already renders the active call stack at the top and Up Next at the bottom. Adding a third progress box in the middle crowds the narrow rail and mishandles loops: ScriptOutlineProjection.ts:104 marks any statement ID with a Round 1 output as completed with strikethrough, so Round 2 12 Pullups shows crossed out right above UP NEXT: 12 Pullups.

8Detail projection cutover and 44px rep targets

Complexity: Deleted 271 lines in wallclock-panel.tsx · Value: High

Deleting StackIntegratedTimer's separate timer-pinning logic in favor of useSessionClockProjection paid down major maintenance debt and unified secondary clock deduplication and the welded Up next caption. Minor polish note: when a Rounds block is already the hero h2 title, rendering the Round 2 of 3 chip underneath repeats the exact same string.

5. Suggested cleanup punch-list for review

Highlight or annotate any item below to mark whether you want to apply it, adjust it, or leave current behavior in place.

  • Stop the 13.5px clock jump on Final 5s. Keep the digit color change in timerPolarityVisuals.tsx, and either remove TimerPolarityBadges plus the conditional 1px container border or reserve fixed vertical space so entering Final 5s, Rest, or Paused never shifts the clock.
  • Fix clipped SkipFlash on Minimal and General. Move SkipFlash inside the centered hero group or drop -translate-y-full at the top edge of TimerCore.tsx:64 so "Timer can't be skipped!" is visible inside overflow-hidden.
  • Cut Script Outline from VisualStatePanel, or make it loop-aware. Right now it duplicates stack and Up Next context while crossing out upcoming movements in Round 2+ via global completedIds in ScriptOutlineProjection.ts:104.
  • Strip enriched analytics fragments from split row labels and scope splits per block. Filter LiveSessionTelemetry.ts:130 to authored movement/rep/load/distance tokens instead of dumping pace, cadence, and MET calculations into the split label, and filter VisualStateComponents.tsx:351 to the enclosing block rather than slicing global telemetry.splits.slice(-4).

  • Deduplicate headers on Fullscreen General and align ADR-0003. Hide GeneralTimerSurface's duplicate workoutTitle when hosted inside FullscreenTimer, suppress the Round X of Y chip when mainLabel already equals roundLabel, and either wire or remove the pocket-remote drawer claim in ADR-0003.
